Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

airoom is a product of applyai OÜ ("we", "our", "us"), a company registered in Estonia. airoom is a desktop application for macOS and Windows that shows your local Claude Code sessions as a pixel-art office, and this policy covers both the app and the airoom.dev website. Questions to hello@airoom.dev.

2. The short version

airoom runs entirely on your machine. Your rooms, transcripts, prompts, code and everything your agents say or do stay there and are never sent to us. The app has no account, no key and no telemetry; the one request it makes on its own is an anonymous check every few hours for a newer build (section 3.3). The website counts visits (3.1), the downloads come from GitHub (3.2), and the only thing we ever store about you is an email address you give us for the waitlist (3.4). This policy is mostly a list of those moments.

3. What we collect, and when

3.1 Visiting airoom.dev

  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages matter. It sets cookies in your browser and shares usage data — pages viewed, rough location derived from your IP, browser and device type — with Google, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No other tracking scripts.
  • Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server request logs (IP address, requested path, time) for security and operations.
  • The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads. That request is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Downloading the app

  • The download buttons send your browser to GitHub, where the installers are published as releases. We ask for nothing — no email, no account. GitHub's own privacy statement covers that request.

3.3 Checking for updates

  • Shortly after it starts, and then every six hours, the app fetches a small public file from airoom.dev that lists the current version and, occasionally, a short announcement, so it can tell you when a new build is out. It is a plain request for a public file: no key, no email, no device identifier and no version number travel with it. Our hosting provider logs it like any other page view (see 3.1).
  • To switch it off, start airoom with AIROOM_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1 in your environment. The app then never contacts airoom.dev on its own.

3.4 The waitlist

  • If you leave your email for the hosted team office, we store it together with the time you joined, send you one email when it opens, and delete it when you ask or once that email has gone out — whichever comes first. No list, no newsletter, no drip campaign.

4. What we never see

Your projects, rooms, transcripts, chats with agents, approvals, code and files. All of it stays on your machine. Your agents talk to Anthropic through your own Claude Code installation under your own account — that traffic does not pass through us and is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

5. Services we rely on

  • Railway — hosting for this website, and for the waitlist and its database. Privacy policy.
  • GitHub — the source code and the installers. Privacy statement.
  • Google Analytics — visit statistics for this website. Privacy policy.

These providers process data on our behalf and under their own safeguards; we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

6. How long we keep things

  • Waitlist entries: until the one email has gone out, or until you ask — whichever is first.
  • Server logs: short-lived and rotated by our hosting provider.

7. Your rights

applyai OÜ is an EU company, so the GDPR applies to everyone we hold data about. You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict processing, and complain to a supervisory authority (in Estonia, the Data Protection Inspectorate — or the authority where you live). Our lawful bases are legitimate interest (visit statistics and keeping the site running) and consent (the address you give us for the waitlist, which you can withdraw by asking us to delete it). California residents: we do not sell personal information, yours included.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@airoom.dev.

8. Children

airoom is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. We will not quietly start collecting more than this page says.
